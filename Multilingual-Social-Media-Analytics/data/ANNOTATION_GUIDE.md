# Human annotation guide
Review the original text in the 200-row sample; do not rely on the supplied template labels.

- English: primarily English wording.
- Hindi: primarily Devanagari Hindi.
- Hinglish: Hindi wording/grammar mainly written in Latin script.
- Sentiment: positive (clear praise), negative (clear complaint), neutral (factual/question without clear polarity).
- Mark sarcasm or unclear context as uncertain in a separate notes column rather than forcing a label.

If possible, ask two reviewers to label independently and resolve disagreements before computing final metrics.
